Beachview Guest House

Beachview is a luxury coastal guest house website designed to advertise rooms, facilities, dining and the local Dornoch area. The website uses a clean layout, large imagery, consistent navigation and interactive features such as a hamburger menu, slideshow, embedded video and contact tools.
